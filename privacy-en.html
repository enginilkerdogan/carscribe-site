Privacy Policy

CarScribe · English

Last updated: November 11, 2026

CarScribe ("the App") is a mobile app that lets vehicle owners keep maintenance, fuel and document records on their own device. This policy explains how the App handles data.

1. Data We Collect

The App does not collect any data that could personally identify you. We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number or location.

The information you enter is stored only on your device:

  • Vehicle details (plate, brand, model, model year, fuel type)
  • Maintenance records (date, odometer, category, cost, notes, interval)
  • Fuel records (date, odometer, liters, price per liter, full-tank flag)
  • Document records (type, expiry date, reminder lead time)
  • Language, currency and notification preferences
  • Premium purchase status (payments are processed by Google Play; card details never reach the App)

These records are never transmitted to us. The App has no server component.

2. Data Security

Records are kept in the app's private storage area on your device, protected by the operating system. Because no data is transferred over the internet, there is no data in transit to encrypt.

3. Advertising (AdMob)

The free version shows Google AdMob banner ads and occasional interstitial ads. The advertising network may process information such as your IP address, device model and ad interactions under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob help: support.google.com/admob

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before any ad is displayed. If you decline, no ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options.

No ads are shown in the Premium version.

4. Purchases

The Premium purchase is processed by Google Play. Your payment details are never entered in the App and are not stored by us; transactions are handled directly by Google Play billing.

5. Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Only the operating system permission is requested; no push messages are sent from any server.

6. Backup and Data Transfer

You can export all data as a JSON file and import the same file back at any time. Those files are entirely under your control and sharing them is entirely your choice.

7. Children

The App is suitable for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal data.

8. Data Deletion

Removing the App from your device deletes its records. You can also clear the app's data from your device settings, and export a backup beforehand if you wish.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is published on this page with its "last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions? Reach us through GitHub Issues.
